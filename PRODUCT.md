# Product

**User.** Someone who needs income and a skill soon: laid off, skipping or leaving college, or changing careers, often in Washington State to start.

**Painful job.** "Which programs will pay me while I train, how much, for how long, and are they real?" Search results mix real apprenticeships with fee-based courses and "money-making opportunities" aimed at people in a hurry.

**Promise.** Only programs that pay (or pay a living allowance), only from official sources, each linked to its source with a date. Nothing that charges job seekers.

**Wedge.** Searches like "paid electrician apprenticeship washington", "apprenticeships near me with starting wage", "get paid to learn a trade", "Job Corps living allowance"; one page per field; an RSS feed of newly registered programs.

**Why now.** Layoff news and AI-driven job changes keep "alternatives to college" and "paid training" searches high, and registered apprenticeship is expanding.

**Activation metric.** A visitor opens a program's official record or apply page (`source-click`, `apply-click`); counted once the GoatCounter key is set.

**First revenue event.** None from job seekers, ever. Possible later: an ethically screened display-ad slot or support link (OPERATIONS.md, key 2).

**What v1 won't do.** List job postings it may not collect, list anything without an official source, show contact details of individuals, or say a program is hiring.
