# Launch

Machine-driven mode: nothing below is posted, sent or bought by this project.

## Live

- https://theguysaccount.github.io/alternative-paths-opportunities/ · feed `/feed.xml` · open data `/data/opportunities.json`

## Custom domain

Owned by the domain rollout session: one repository variable (`SITE_URL`) plus the Pages domain (OPERATIONS.md, key 1).

## Machine distribution running

- A page per occupational field ("Paid construction apprenticeships in Washington: wages and programs") with ItemList / EducationalOccupationalProgram and Breadcrumb structured data, plus program pages with Article data.
- IndexNow after every deploy; the account's host-root robots.txt lists the sitemap.
- RSS of newly registered and expired programs; share buttons; open JSON.

"paid apprenticeships washington state", "electrician apprenticeship washington starting wage", "lineman apprenticeship washington pay", "medical assistant apprenticeship washington", "get paid to learn a trade", "job corps living allowance", "americorps living allowance", "us digital corps salary", "help after a layoff training".

## First 1,000 visitors (machine-only)

Field pages and program pages rank for wage and "paid apprenticeship" searches; the daily refresh keeps them fresh for crawlers; each new state source the routine adds is a new set of entry points. No posts, outreach or ads.
